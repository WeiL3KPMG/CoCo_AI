Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China, with a nationwide service network across Mainland China and physical tailor stores in major Chinese cities. The company also has stores in Hong Kong and is expanding its custom clothing services to selected overseas and international customer segments.

Sector:
Custom Apparel / Fashion Technology / C2M E-Commerce / Online-to-Offline Tailoring / Smart Manufacturing / Made-to-Measure Clothing

Core Business Description:
YBREN, also branded internationally as YBR.Tailor and known in Chinese as 衣邦人, is a China-based custom clothing and fashion technology company that provides made-to-measure apparel through an online-to-offline service model. Founded in 2014 in Hangzhou, the company focuses on personalised clothing customization, including men’s suits, shirts, business formalwear, women’s formalwear, professional attire, casualwear, and other full-category custom apparel.

YBREN pioneered a C2M model in China’s custom clothing industry by combining internet-based ordering, professional door-to-door body measurement, offline tailor consultation, and Industry 4.0-enabled production. Customers can make appointments online, after which trained styling consultants or tailors visit customers at their home, office, hotel, or preferred location to collect body measurements and provide styling advice. Orders are then routed through YBREN’s digital manufacturing and supply chain systems for customised production.

The company’s value proposition is to make high-quality custom clothing more accessible, convenient, and cost-effective by removing traditional retail intermediaries, integrating global fabric sourcing, using digital body measurement data, and applying intelligent manufacturing systems. YBREN positions itself as a large-scale custom clothing retail brand that combines fashion advisory services, advanced manufacturing, data-driven sizing, and personalised customer experience.

Business Model:
O2O / C2M custom apparel model combining online customer acquisition, offline measurement services, and digitally enabled manufacturing.

YBREN allows customers to book tailoring appointments online or through its app / platform. Professional consultants provide free or low-cost door-to-door measurement and styling services, collect detailed body data, and recommend suitable fabrics, cuts, and clothing styles. The company then uses its digital garment systems and supply chain network to produce customised apparel at scale.

The company appears to generate revenue primarily through direct sales of customised garments, including suits, shirts, businesswear, women’s formalwear, casualwear, and corporate clothing. Its business model is supported by repeat customer purchases, wardrobe expansion, higher-value fabric options, corporate or group orders, and full-category customization. YBREN’s use of direct-to-consumer channels, data-driven manufacturing, and global fabric sourcing allows it to offer customised clothing at a lower price point than traditional luxury tailoring while maintaining a premium positioning.
