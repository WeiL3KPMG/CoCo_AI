Company Name:
Marketnode 

Headquarters / Primary Geography:
Singapore (with primary focus on Singapore and broader Asia-Pacific capital markets) 

Sector:
Digital Market Infrastructure / Capital Markets Infrastructure / Financial Technology 

Core Business Description:
Marketnode is a Singapore-based digital market infrastructure operator focused on modernising capital markets workflows across funds, credit, and structured products. The company provides institutional infrastructure built on distributed ledger / blockchain technology to help financial institutions automate, integrate, and distribute traditional and tokenized products. Its offerings span data services, transaction management, and tokenization / registry capabilities, positioning the firm as a neutral infrastructure layer for post-trade processing, servicing, and digital asset enablement in Asia-Pacific. 

Business Segments:
- Funds market infrastructure (Fundnode)
- Credit / fixed income digital market infrastructure
- Structured products infrastructure
- Data services (conversion of unstructured data into structured downstream data)
- Transaction management (subscriptions, redemptions, order management, corporate actions, transfer-agent-style workflows)
- Tokenization and registry / asset servicing capabilities (including Gateway) 

Business Model:
B2B, institutional infrastructure model. Marketnode provides shared market infrastructure and workflow solutions to financial institutions and ecosystem participants such as fund managers, distributors, transfer agents, service providers, issuers, banks, and investors. The platform is designed to reduce manual processing, improve reconciliation and record-keeping, and enable issuance, distribution, settlement, and servicing of traditional and tokenized financial products.
