TARGET COMPANY PROFILE – REFERENCE FOR COCO COMPARISON

Company Name:
Greenko Group

Headquarters / Primary Geography:
India (operations across multiple Indian states)

Sector:
Renewable Energy and Energy Transition Infrastructure

Core Business Description:
Greenko Group is a renewable energy infrastructure company focused on developing, owning, and operating large-scale renewable power assets in India. Its portfolio spans wind, solar, and hydro generation, with a strategic emphasis on integrating energy storage to deliver firm and dispatchable renewable power. The company positions itself as an energy transition and decarbonization solutions provider rather than a pure power generator.

Business Segments:
- Wind power generation
- Solar power generation
- Hydro power generation
- Energy storage (pumped storage and battery-based solutions)
- Emerging green hydrogen and green ammonia initiatives

Business Model:
B2B, asset-heavy infrastructure model. Greenko develops and operates renewable and storage assets and supplies power and decarbonization solutions primarily to industrial, commercial, and grid counterparties. Revenue is driven by long-term power offtake, contracts, and structured energy solutions rather than retail sales.

Scale Indicators (Approximate):
- Installed renewable capacity: ~7.5 GW
- Energy storage projects under development: 50 GWh near-term, scaling to ~100 GWh
- Geographic footprint: Multi-state presence within India

Strategic Positioning:
- Focus on dispatchable renewable energy through integrated storage
- Positioned as a deep decarbonization and energy transition platform
- Emphasis on reliability and grid-scale solutions, not just generation capacity
